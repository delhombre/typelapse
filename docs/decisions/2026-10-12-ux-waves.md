# UX after the design pass: what Polar and Dub teach, in two waves

Bruno, 2026-10-12, after testing staging: "je ne trouve pas l'expérience
utilisateur folle"; look at Polar, Dub and competitors, borrow what improves
onboarding, payment and product understanding, and plan tickets if the weekly
budget runs out. Research: [docs/research/2026-10-12-ux-benchmark.md]
(../research/2026-10-12-ux-benchmark.md). Fable decides as PO and CTO.

## D1 Borrow their guidance and upgrade moments, never their sign-up wall

**Decision:** Typelapse keeps its advantage, a studio that works at once with
no account and no upload. From Polar and Dub we take the opening fork, the
next-step card, the upgrade prompt on the pressed control, the usage meter
and the return from checkout that says what changed. Nothing new is gated,
no watermark, no limit on device exports.

**Why:** Polar and Dub are dashboards that gate everything behind sign-up;
snipcast.dev gates even the trial. Our first minute is already better than
theirs; what is missing is telling the visitor what to do next, what an
account adds, and that a paid side exists.

## D2 Two waves, cheapest first

**Decision:** wave 1 is the five S items, shipped as two PRs: the empty-state
fork with the account-value copy and the sandbox line (#250, #252, #258), then
the checkout return sheet with the usage meter (#257, #255). Wave 2 is the M
items in this order: the paid side visible under Where (#254), the first-video
card (#251), the pricing page (#256, numbers wait on #79), the help sheet and
what's new (#259, #260), the account sheet with tabs (#261).

**Why:** the S items sit on the screen every visitor sees and on the screen a
buyer sees after paying, and they are copy and placement, not systems. The
budget this week does not cover wave 2.

**Rejected for now:**

- "Continue with GitHub" (#253): passwordless-auth D1 already deferred OAuth
  to the GitHub App. It stays the first candidate once that app ships.

## D3 The paid side is visible to everyone, gated only at the action

**Decision:** in the hosted build, Cloud always appears under Where. Signed
out, pressing it explains what a hosted render adds and offers sign-in; on
Free, it shows the allowance and one "Upgrade to Creator". The captions
caveat links to the same sheet. The device export stays free and whole.

**Why:** today a free visitor who adds a caption and exports on device gets a
video without it and never learns hosted renders bake it. That is the moment
revenue is decided, and it currently offers no way forward.
