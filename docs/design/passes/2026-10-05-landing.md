## Decisions

Foundations (`emil-design-foundations`):

- Static frames lay their lines out as a flex column (`.studio-code code`),
  which drops the whitespace text, and each `.line` keeps `min-height: 1lh`.
  Leading is now 15.2px for 9.5px code, 16.8px for 10.5px (measured).
- Hero meta: each fact is a `whitespace-nowrap` span and the separator is held
  to the fact before it (`hero.tsx`, `META`), so a wrap never splits a fact
  or opens a line on a dot.
- `.lp-h2` 700 to 600, the display's weight. Size, tracking and balance kept.
- One plain sentence per section line: diff ("Paste a unified diff or a
  GitHub pull request, and Typelapse turns the change into editable scenes on
  the timeline."), social, export ("One clip, five cuts: ..."). The morph line
  kept: its second sentence is the WYSIWYG claim. No claim, number or price
  changed; "seven themes" still stands in the spec sheet.
- CTA heading "Paste a change and make your first clip.", so the button is
  the only place that says "Open the studio".
- Spec sheet values in sentence case with a full stop.
- `.lp-island-caption` takes `text-wrap: balance`.
- Kept: no headline in the hero (decision 2026-08-09), the numbered steps,
  the 80 / 96 / 112 rhythm, the 17px lead, the blue focus ring (D9).

Material (`emil-marketing-pages`): the page already passes its checklist,
nothing changed for it. Intro once per session (`sessionStorage`), no
scroll-triggered entrance (non-immediate `Reveal` is a plain `div`), Geist
and Ioskeley preloaded, nothing advances without the visitor, CTA right for
signed-in and signed-out alike (no account needed).

Unslop (`emil-unslop-design`), subtract first:

- The sparkle on the ready badge becomes three clips on a track
  (`ScenesIcon`), the thing the badge reports.
- The second, tighter glow behind the phone is gone; the section keeps one.
- The export chip row is gone (and `Chip` with it): the line names the
  formats, the fan shows the shapes. The fan sits 40px under the line.
- Fan cards carry `users.filter(\n  (u) => u.active\n)` and no line-number
  gutter, so the 9:16 card (180px) holds every line whole. Exported as
  `FAN_CODE`, `FAN_FONT`, `FAN_FRAME_PADDING`, `FAN_MESH_PADDING`, tested.
- Diff shot at 520px and under: no gutter, and the code steps from 9.5 to
  8.25px through `--frame-font`, which `StaticFrame` now reads before its
  `fontSize` prop, so CSS can size it without fighting an inline style.
- Kept: the fan's hover lift. A card is picked up to be read; it is the one
  playful gesture on the page and it is measured (see Motion).

Polish (`emil-ui-polish`):

- `.lp-mode[data-active]` no longer changes weight; ink and the surface fill
  carry the state. Measured after: 100.4 / 48.8 / 74.9px in every mode.
- Platform chips: unselected `hover:border-white/20 hover:text-white/70`
  (Tailwind v4 gates hover to hover devices) and `active:scale-[0.97]`.
- Kept: press at 0.97 (the project's `PRESS_SCALE`; Craft agrees), the blue
  focus ring (the primary fails 3:1 on paper, D9).

Motion (`emil-animations`; `review-animations` is not installed in this
session, its review checklist ran from `emil-animations`):

- Reduced motion has no intro at all: `LandingIntroProvider` answers false
  under `prefers-reduced-motion`, so every `Reveal`, `RevealGroup` and
  `RevealItem` renders a plain block. It cross-faded for 400ms before;
  `REVEAL_DURATION_REDUCED` is deleted. Stricter than D8, which allows
  opacity: this is copy that is ready on the first frame.
- "Play motion" is not rendered under reduced motion.
- `LiveMorph`'s padding reader treats an unparsable padding as 0 (a NaN room
  refitted forever in jsdom; found writing the test).

Craft (`craft-design-engineering`):

- "Play motion" takes `.hit-ext` (36px tall, 44px target).
- `.lp-island-button:active` 0.96 to 0.97, the page's one press value. This
  settles the landing half of `SYSTEM.md` Drift 7; the studio's three 0.96
  rules remain (`.si-button`, `.si-cta`, `.sc-glyph-button`).
- Checked and kept: concentric radii on the fan (outer = 6 + mesh padding),
  inset outline on the sharpness crops, tabular numbers on every count and
  timecode, `html` painted per theme.

Orchestrator's calls after the review, applied the same day:

- **One filled "Open studio" per viewport.** The hero field's button stays
  the primary. The instrument island sits inside the hero, so whenever it is
  on screen the field is too: its CTA is now quiet there (`IslandCta`,
  `data-quiet`): transparent, `--dock-ink`, the same 30px box and `.hit-ext`
  target, hover one step of white fill (`rgb(255 255 255 / 0.07)`, D4). The
  hero passes `quietCta`; anywhere else the instrument's CTA stays filled.
  The filled one past the hero is the nav island's, which `DynamicNav` keeps
  hidden until the hero has scrolled out, at every width. This amends D2
  ("the island's CTA is the zone's one filled shape") for the hero only.
  The CTA's own press went from 0.98 to 0.97 with it (Drift 7).
- **The aspect change no longer animates width or height (D8).** The frame is
  one box the size of every ratio at once (`frameBox`: 636 × 366 at full
  scale) and the ratio is a `clip-path: inset(... round 18px)` on it
  (`frameClip`), driven by the same `gentle` spring (stiffness 120, damping
  18) through `useTransform`. A clip cuts its element's own shadow, so each
  ratio keeps a still shadow layer of its own size underneath; the outgoing
  one fades out in 120ms, the incoming one fades in over 350ms after 200ms,
  once the clip has nearly filled it. An even cross-fade showed the larger
  shadow's empty middle as a pale card round the frame mid-spring (seen on
  the shot, fixed). A resize jumps instead of springing.
  Trace of one 16:9 to 1:1 change with the morph paused
  (`scratchpad/132/perf.mjs`, `devtools.timeline`): **172 Layout events,
  13.4ms, from the click to 1.43s** before; **7 events, 1.2ms** after. The 7
  are the click itself (the label text, the helper tooltip mounting), the
  start and end of the shadow fade, and three 0.1ms passes on the island's
  aspect glyph; none per frame of the spring.

Orchestrator's direction calls (Drift 1, 3, 6, 11): all four make the landing
the reference (its primary, its warm paper, its instrument island, its
floating material), so nothing on the landing changes for them. The studio
side belongs to the studio desktop pass; this pass did not touch the studio.
